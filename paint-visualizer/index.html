See your home in a new color

Pick a room or elevation, tap any surface, and try colors on it. Or upload a photo of your own house and we'll paint that instead — nothing leaves your browser.

  • 1. Pick a surfaceTap the siding, trim, door or cabinets right on the image.
  • 2. Try colorsBrowse the palette, search by name, or use any custom color.
  • 3. CompareHold Before / After and drag to wipe between the two.
  • 4. Send it overSave the image or share the link so we can quote it.

Demo scenes are drawn in code, not photographed — they exist so this page works with no assets. Replace them with your own job photos using the studio, which writes out a scene file you drop into scenes/. See README.md for embedding instructions.
