Scene Studio

Turn one of your job photos into a scene visitors can repaint. Everything happens in this browser tab — the photo is never uploaded anywhere.

  1. Click + Use my photo and choose a photo of a house or room.
  2. Tap a surface — the siding, a wall, the cabinet doors. Move Sensitivity if it grabbed too much or too little.
  3. Use Add / Remove to brush in the bits it missed (behind a downspout, under a gutter).
  4. Rename it to what it is: Siding, Trim, Front Door, Upper Cabinets. The name sets the surface's role, so designer schemes apply correctly.
  5. Repeat for each paintable surface, then click Export Scene.
  6. Drop the downloaded .json into scenes/ and list it in your sceneFiles config.
What makes a good scene photo: shot square-on in even light (overcast is ideal), the whole elevation in frame, no hard shadows cutting across the siding, and nothing parked in front. The engine keeps whatever shading is in the photo — so a clean photo repaints cleanly, and a photo with a tree shadow across the wall will keep that shadow in every color.
